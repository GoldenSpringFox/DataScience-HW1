# Task 6.4 — Functional role classification
Date: 2026-08-16 | Status: done (vocabulary revised three times same day after user review — §Revision, §Revision 2, §Revision 3)

## Goal & hypothesis

Give every card in the pool one **primary** and an optional **secondary** functional role from a
fixed 15-role vocabulary (`ramp, card_draw, spot_removal, board_wipe, counterspell, tutor,
recursion, graveyard_hate, protection, evasion_enabler, wincon, stax_tax, land, synergy_piece,
other`), so later tasks can talk about a deck as *role counts* rather than as a bag of cards.
This is what task 6.6's archetype role quotas ("this Krenko archetype runs 9–12 ramp, 6–8 draw")
and the cut recommender's "you are three lands short and long on 6-drops" reasoning both need.

Hypothesis going in: because task 5.5 already imported Scryfall Tagger's functional tags **with
their ancestor chains expanded**, the tag layer alone should carry most cards, and the oracle-text
heuristics would be a thin backstop for the untagged tail. That held — with one correction: two
roles (`wincon`, `stax_tax`) have no broad Tagger anchor and genuinely need the text layer.

Module: `edhcut/analysis/roles.py`. Output: `data/kb/dev/roles.parquet` (plan §2.4).

## Method & design decisions

Three layers, in the plan's order: (1) tagger tags via an explicit mapping dict, (2) regex
heuristics over normalized oracle text, (3) a default.

### Weighted scoring, not first-match-wins

Real cards carry several role signals at once, and the right answer is usually "the strongest one,
plus a real second". Path to Exile is tagged `ramp`, `tutor` **and** `spot-removal`; Cyclonic Rift
is tagged both `spot-removal` and `sweeper`. So each role accumulates a score and the argmax wins,
with the runner-up falling out for free as the secondary. Weight scale is deliberately coarse:
**~3–5 for an anchor tag** that alone justifies the role, **0.5–1.0 for a corroborator**, negative
for an explicit correction.

### Anchor tags only, never the full expansion

Because 5.5 expanded ancestors, `removal` is a parent of both `spot-removal` and `sweeper`, and a
wordy card like Cyclonic Rift carries **ten** `removal-<permanent type>` tags. Summing all of them
would let verbosity beat meaning, so each role's rule set is a small number of anchors plus a few
low-weight corroborators. Verified the hierarchy empirically before writing the rules rather than
assuming it (`sweeper`→`removal` 100%, `spot-removal`→`removal` 100%, `mana-rock`→`ramp` 100%,
`reanimate`→`recursion` 100%) — which also caught two places where the obvious assumption is
**false**: `counterspell` is *not* under `removal` (10.1%), and `gives-trample` is *not* under
`gives-evasion` (5.2%, so trample is listed separately).

### Negative weights

Used where a child tag means the *opposite* of what its parent implies:

- `tutor-land-to-battlefield: -3.0` and `tutor-land-basic: -1.5` — fetching a land is ramp, not
  tutoring. Cultivate carries both `ramp` and `tutor`; the penalty exactly cancels `tutor` (3.0) +
  `tutor-to-battlefield` (0.5) so nothing leaks through even as a secondary. Bare `tutor-land` is
  deliberately **not** penalised: Expedition Map fetching a utility land is a genuine tutor.
- `donate-rampant-growth: -4.0` on `ramp` — the searched land goes to an *opponent*. That is Path
  to Exile's drawback, not a mode you play it for.
- `recursion-self: -2.5` — a card that only returns *itself* (Bloodghast) is a synergy piece, not
  a recursion spell you slot in for the effect.

### `land` is a type-line override, not a scored role

Anything whose `type_line` contains "Land" is `land` primary at a dominant score, and its
functional role becomes the **secondary**: Bojuka Bog → land / graveyard_hate, Ancient Tomb → land
/ ramp, Rogue's Passage → land / evasion_enabler. This is what makes role counts usable as deck
quotas in 6.6 — a land that also ramps must not be counted out of the mana base.

### Two thresholds

- **`MIN_ROLE_SCORE = 1.0`** — a role needs an anchor, two corroborators, or one text rule; never a
  lone 0.5. See *Impediments*: this was added after the pool-wide distribution exposed the bug it
  fixes.
- **`SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE = 1.5`** — a runner-up needs more than one incidental signal. Tuned
  against Craterhoof Behemoth, which grants trample (1.0) but is not an evasion enabler.

Ties are broken by a declared `ROLE_PRIORITY` order so the output is deterministic regardless of
dict iteration order.

### Layer 3 default: `synergy_piece` vs `other`

Rather than enumerate what makes a card a synergy piece, the rule inverts: a `NON_FUNCTIONAL_TAGS`
set plus four patterns (`^cycle`, `vanilla`, `^deprecated-`, `^aesthetic-`) covers naming/flavour
trivia, printing cycles, and the two purely structural "has an ability" tags. A card with **any**
tag outside that set is a `synergy_piece` ("does something, just nothing on our role list" — Krenko
makes tokens); a card whose entire tag set falls inside it, or which has no tags, is `other`
("we genuinely learned nothing").

### Rejected: summing the two layers

The first build summed layer 1 and layer 2 per role. This is double counting — a draw spell tagged
`draw` whose text also says "draw a card" is **one** piece of evidence observed twice — and it
showed: Mind Stone (a mana rock that can be sacrificed to draw) scored ramp 4.0+2.0 against
card_draw 4.0+3.0 and came out a *draw spell*. Switched to **max**, on the principle that the
layers are two independent readings of the same card, which also keeps a one-anchor card
comparable to any other one-anchor card whichever layer saw it. Fixed Mind Stone (→ ramp /
card_draw) at a cost of 2 points of spot-check accuracy, both on genuinely dual-role cards.

## Interesting findings

**The wincon list is the one to show in the report.** Only 150 cards, and reading the ones that
appear in ≥30 harvested decks it is genuinely a list of ways to win: Overwhelming Stampede,
Craterhoof Behemoth, Laboratory Maniac, Triumph of the Hordes, Thassa's Oracle, Approach of the
Second Sun, Overrun, Mechanized Production, Revel in Riches, Felidar Sovereign, Test of Endurance,
Simic Ascendancy, Darksteel Reactor, and a long tail of extra-turn spells (Time Warp, Nexus of
Fate, Temporal Manipulation, Capture of Jingzhou, Expropriate). This is the role the tags were
*worst* equipped for — its three anchors cover ~190 cards total — and it came out cleanest.

**The role mix of the 60 most-played cards reads as a Commander deck.** Sol Ring → ramp, Command
Tower → land, Swiftfoot Boots → protection, Swords to Plowshares → spot_removal, Skullclamp →
card_draw, Blasphemous Act → board_wipe, Eternal Witness → recursion, Goblin Matron → tutor.
The dual-role cards are the pleasing part: Rogue's Passage → land / evasion_enabler, Myriad
Landscape → land / ramp, Bojuka Bog → land / graveyard_hate, Mind Stone → ramp / card_draw,
Vandalblast → board_wipe / spot_removal, Farseek → ramp / tutor.

**Two failure modes are visible and are taxonomy limits, not bugs**, both worth a sentence in the
report: (1) goblin creatures with a removal ability (Siege-Gang Commander, Goblin Trashmaster,
Pashalik Mons) classify as `spot_removal` when a player would call them typal payoffs; (2) lords
that grant evasion (Goblin King, Champion of Lambholt) classify as `evasion_enabler`. In both
cases the assigned role is *true*, it just isn't the most *characteristic* thing about the card.
The role list has no `sacrifice_outlet` and no `anthem`/`lord`, so there is nowhere truer to put
them.

**Farseek and Nature's Lore never use the word "land"** — "search your library for a Plains,
Island, Swamp, or Mountain card". Any text rule written the obvious way reads them as generic
tutors. The fix (spelling out basic land *type names*) is now a single `_LAND_TARGET` fragment
shared by the `ramp` rule that wants it and the `tutor` rule that must exclude it, so the two can
never disagree about what counts as fetching a land.

## Impediments

**A lone 0.5-weight corroborator could become a primary role, and no test would have caught it.**
The 60-card spot-check passed at 98% while `wincon` held **843** cards pool-wide against the ~190
its anchors cover: `power-boost-to-all` (0.5, and under no parent this mapping uses) was making
every anthem effect a wincon, simply by being the only role that scored at all. Fixed with
`MIN_ROLE_SCORE = 1.0`; wincon dropped **843 → 150**. This is the project's standing lesson landing
again — *unit tests on a formula's mechanics don't substitute for eyeballing real output* — and it
is now the fifth confirmed instance. Nothing about the spot-check could have found it, because the
bug only affected cards with **no** strong signal, and every card in a labeled set of famous cards
has one.

**Two regexes double-scoring the same clause.** `gains? (...)` and a redundant `gain (...)` both
fired on "creatures you control gain trample", doubling the score and pushing Craterhoof Behemoth
from `wincon` to `evasion_enabler`; the same pattern in `draws?` vs `draw` inflated every draw
spell. Both were invisible until the spot-check named a specific wrong card. Now covered by tests
asserting the exact score of a single clause.

**Necropotence classified as `graveyard_hate`** — its text-rule matched "exile that card from
**your** graveyard". Exiling from your own graveyard is a cost or a fuel engine, never hate; the
rule now carries a `(?!your\b)` lookahead. It then had *no* draw signal at all (Tagger tags it
`card-advantage`/`repeatable-card-advantage` but not `draw`, and the card never uses the word —
it exiles cards to hand), which is why `repeatable-card-advantage` is mapped: the pair clears
`MIN_ROLE_SCORE` and catches draw engines that never say "draw".

**One mapped tag didn't exist.** `static-ability` is not in the live vocabulary. `unknown_tags()`
exists precisely for this — a typo'd slug reads like a rule but contributes nothing — and it runs
on every `build`.

Cost: roughly one full re-measure cycle per issue. All four were found by *looking at output*
(distribution table, top-cards-per-role listing, spot-check misses), none by a test.

## Revision — vocabulary overhaul (same day, after user review of the real output)

The first build's role list came straight from the plan's task prompt. Reviewing the actual
classifications, the user revised the vocabulary itself. Everything above still describes the
mechanism; this section is what changed and what it cost. Notebook:
`notebooks/roles_exploration.ipynb` (built for this review — worked examples, the top 100
most-played cards as images, the tricky cases, and the user's own Kyler decklist by role).

**Renames**: `card_draw`→`draw`, `board_wipe`→`boardwipe`, `evasion_enabler`→`evasion`,
`stax_tax`→`stax`, and `counterspell`→**`stack_interaction`**, broadened past countering to
copying (`copy-spell`), redirecting (`change-target`), granting flash (`gives-flash`), and denying
opponents the stack (`prevent-cast`, `prevent-activation` — Grand Abolisher).

**Four roles added**: `sacrifice_outlet` (Tagger's `sacrifice-outlet` covers 1,382 cards, so this
was nearly free — and it resolves the first build's one non-tie-break spot-check miss, Ashnod's
Altar); `defensive` (keeping *you* alive, as opposed to `protection` keeping a *permanent* alive —
fogs, lifegain, pillowfort, goad); and `land_destruction` / `mass_land_destruction` split out of
spot_removal/boardwipe, with **MLD taking precedence over boardwipe** by decision.

**Anthems now score `wincon`** (`anthem` 3.0) — the first build's second known vocabulary gap,
closed. `keyword-anthem` and `power-boost-to-all` are corroborators only, because both also sit on
temporary combat pumps.

**`synergy_piece` dropped.** `other` is now the only default. A role vocabulary should name what a
card does or admit it doesn't, and `synergy_piece` was doing neither for 12,841 cards.

**Impulse draw is now an anchor** (`impulsive-draw` 3.0, `impulse` 1.5). Tagger never tags Light Up
the Stage or Outpost Siege `draw` — correctly, they never draw — so before this they had no draw
signal at all and fell through to the default.

### The trap this revision surfaced

**Tagger's `mass-land-denial` is about land *denial*, not destruction.** 25+ cards carry it with
no `removal-land` at all: Winter Orb, Blood Moon, Stasis, Back to Basics, Contamination, Static
Orb. Those are stax. Using it as the MLD anchor made Winter Orb and Blood Moon mass land
destruction. The fix needs **both** tags, neither sufficient alone, plus `spot-removal: -4.0` (a
card that hits one thing cannot be *mass* anything — Chaos Warp carries `removal-land`), and
`mass-land-denial` moved to `stax` where it belongs.

The mirror problem hit `land_destruction`: `removal-land` also sits on every "destroy target
permanent" card. Discriminator, found by diffing real tag sets: **the other `removal-<type>`
tags**. Stone Rain and Strip Mine carry `removal-land` and nothing else; Acidic Slime, Decimate,
Terastodon and Imprisoned in the Moon each carry two or three more and are multi-mode removal that
happens to list land. Each of those is now −1.5. Separately, `hate-nonbasic-land` at 1.0 was enough
to clear the evidence floor alone, making Thalia Heretic Cathar and Archon of Emeria "land
destruction" — dropped to 0.5 and added to `stax` at 2.0. Land destruction went from 216 cards
(visibly polluted) to **126** (clean on inspection).

Two text rules needed the same `(?!your\b)` treatment graveyard_hate already had, for the same
reason — **hitting your own resource is a cost, not removal**: `(?! card)` keeps land destruction
off Deathrite Shaman's "exile target land card from a graveyard", and `(?! you control)` keeps it
off Extraplanar Lens's imprint.

## Revision 2 — the vocabulary settles (same day, second user review)

Reviewing the notebook, the user made four more calls. Final vocabulary: **17 roles**.

**Land destruction is not its own role after all.** Single-target land destruction merges into
`spot_removal`; **mass land destruction is classed as `stax`**. Both merges made the code
*smaller*, which is the interesting part — the separate roles had been fighting the tag data the
whole time. `mass_land_destruction` needed two conjoined tags plus a `spot-removal: -4.0` penalty
to keep Chaos Warp out; routing it to `stax` needs none of that, because `mass-land-denial`'s real
meaning (denial, covering both Winter Orb and Armageddon) *is* stax. `land_destruction` needed
five negative weights to separate Stone Rain from Acidic Slime; as a `spot_removal` corroborator
it needs zero. Eight rules deleted, no capability lost.

One thing was worth keeping from the deleted roles: **`removal-creature` separates Armageddon from
Jokulhaups**. Both are stax, but only Jokulhaups also wipes a board, and it keeps `boardwipe` as
its secondary while Armageddon (which carries `sweeper` but destroys no creatures) gets none.

**New role `board_presence`** — bodies that can block or attack, plus anything that just adds a
pile of stats. It is the first role a card can earn from its **type line alone**: a vanilla
creature is a body, so `CREATURE_SCORE = 1.0`, chosen to sit exactly at `MIN_ROLE_SCORE` but
*below* `SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE`. That one value is doing real work — it lets `board_presence` answer
when nothing else does, without stapling "/ board_presence" onto all 17,751 creatures as a noise
secondary. Tag anchors on top: `repeatable-creature-tokens` 3.0, `gives-pp-counters` 2.0,
`gains-pp-counters` 1.5, `power-boost-to-all`/`toughness-boost-to-all` 1.0. Artifact and Treasure
tokens are deliberately excluded — a Treasure cannot block.

### Two bugs this round, both from a tag meaning something other than its name

**`CREATURE_SCORE` initially lived in `score_text`, and broke the agreement metric.** Agreement
fell 80.4% → **62.9%** in one step, not because classification got worse but because the
"heuristic layer" now fired on every creature in the pool, so `heuristic_primary` was
`board_presence` for thousands of cards the tagger read correctly. Fixed by applying type-line
signals in `classify` instead, alongside the `land` override, and adding `type_line` as a fourth
value of `primary_source`. Agreement recovered to **76.6%**. Worth stating as a general point:
**a metric over "two independent layers" silently stops measuring that the moment a signal is
added to the wrong layer** — and the classification looked fine throughout.

**Tagger's `tax` is not stax.** At 3.0 it was a stax anchor; it turns out Tagger uses `tax` (with
`toll`) for "something happens when an opponent does something", which covers Rhystic Study but
also **Soul Warden, Essence Warden, Roaming Throne and Taurean Mauler** — all of which came out as
stax pieces. Demoted to 0.5, with `cast-tax` and `cost-increaser` promoted to 3.0 as the real
anchors: those name an actual cost being imposed. Soul Warden → `defensive`, Roaming Throne →
`board_presence`, Rhystic Study unchanged at `draw / stax`.

That is now **three** tags in this task whose name misled: `mass-land-denial`, `removal-land`, and
`tax`. General rule, added to the project's memory: *before using a Tagger tag as a role anchor,
list the cards that carry it without the tag you assume co-occurs.*

## Revision 3 — the vocabulary settles at 18 roles (third user review)

Fourteen more calls from the user, reviewing the notebook. Two of them needed **new mechanisms**;
the rest were weights.

**Two new roles.** `enhancer` — doublers, increasers, extra phases, counter manipulation (Doubling
Season, Panharmonicon, Hardened Scales, The Ozolith, Thousand-Year Elixir, Paradox Haze). This is
exactly the payoff/multiplier group revision 2 identified as the leftover in `other`, so the role
was proposed by the data and named by the user. And `board_presence` was **narrowed**: the blanket
creature floor is gone, replaced by token generation, lords, +1/+1 counter distribution, and a
genuine big-body signal read straight off `cards.power`/`toughness` (≥5 in either → 2.5; `*` does
not count, because that size belongs to the board, not the card). A vanilla 2/2 and a generic
Bonesplitter now get nothing. The role fell from 10,028 primaries to 4,945.

**Land destruction, third attempt, and now correct.** Not its own role, and not simply
`spot_removal` either — it splits on whether the card gives anything back:

- **`stax`** when it does not: Stone Rain, Wasteland, Molten Rain, and all mass land destruction.
- **`spot_removal`** when it either replaces the land (`swap-removal` — Ghost Quarter, Demolition
  Field) or could have hit a nonland permanent instead (other `removal-<type>` tags — Beast Within,
  Acidic Slime, Chaos Warp).

Both escape hatches are single tags, so the whole distinction is six weights in the `stax` rule set.
This is the third framing of this one question across three revisions, and the only one that both
matches how the cards play and needs no special-case machinery.

**Other calls, all straightforward once the mechanisms existed**: `ramp` → **`mana_acceleration`**
(so rituals stop reading wrong) now including **cost reducers** (`cost-reducer`, 292 cards) and
still excluding colour fixing — which needed no code at all, since Prismatic Omen carries `mana-fix`
but no `ramp`; **cantrips out of `draw`** via `cantrip`/`hand-neutral` penalties, which also removes
Mind Stone and Commander's Sphere; **loot and rummage into `draw`** (`loot` 383 cards, `rummage`
265); **damage aimed at players into `wincon`** (`burn-player` 4.0, with `burn-creature` −3.0 as the
discriminator that keeps Lightning Bolt in removal); **extra turns and extra combats into `wincon`**;
**"can't be countered" into `stack_interaction`** (`gives-uncounterable` — Cavern of Souls);
**`protection` restricted to your own permanents**, demoting the `protection` tag to 1.5 and
promoting `protects-*` (this is what stops Solitary Confinement, which protects the *player*);
**pillowfort confirmed as `defensive`**; and `SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE` lowered 1.5 → **1.0** on the
instruction to be liberal with secondaries.

### Two mechanisms the weights could not express

**Cross-layer penalties.** Penalties are now applied *after* the two layers are combined, not folded
into the tag layer. Ponder is the proof: `cantrip` + `hand-neutral` cancel its tag-layer `draw`, but
the text layer independently reads a plain "Draw a card." and carried the role through regardless. A
negative weight is a claim about the **card**, not about a layer, so it has to apply to whichever
layer noticed the role. `_tag_components` splits credits from penalties for exactly this.

**Tag conjunctions (`TAG_COMBOS`).** Additive weights cannot say "A and B together mean something
neither means alone," and the user's lord rule needs precisely that: a lord is `board_presence`, but
a lord that *also grants evasion* is a `wincon`. With plain weights the only way to promote Eldrazi
Monument and Lord of the Accursed would be a large `gives-evasion` weight on `wincon`, which would
then fire on all ~1,000 evasion granters in the pool. Three combos exist:
`{anthem, gives-evasion} → wincon +5.0`, `{anthem, quadratic} → wincon +4.0`, and
`{sweeper, removal-creature} → boardwipe +1.0`.

That last one is also a bug fix worth recording. `removal-creature` had been a plain 1.0 weight on
`boardwipe`, which was harmless at `SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE = 1.5` — but the moment the threshold
dropped to 1.0, all 5,219 cards carrying that tag gained a spurious `boardwipe` **secondary** (2,597
of them). **Lowering a threshold re-audits every corroborator weight in the mapping**, and the only
thing that caught it was the pool-wide secondary count.

### One request that could not be met

**Skullclamp's secondary should be `sacrifice_outlet`, and there is no signal for it.** Skullclamp
carries no sacrifice tag and its oracle text never says "sacrifice" — it kills X/1 creatures as a
side effect of a −1 toughness. Neither layer can see that, so it is `draw` with no secondary. This
is a genuine limit of tag-plus-text classification rather than a tuning miss, and it is the clearest
example in the task of what a purely mechanical reading of a card cannot recover.

- ~~The role vocabulary has two real gaps: `sacrifice_outlet` and `anthem`/`lord`.~~ **Both closed
  in the same-day revision above** — `sacrifice_outlet` is now a role, and anthems score `wincon`.

- **`SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE` is now 1.0, so every 1.0-weight corroborator can stand alone as a
  secondary.** If it is ever lowered again, re-audit every weight in that band first — that is what
  produced 2,597 spurious `boardwipe` secondaries in revision 3.
- **Task 6.5 must not feed its mined tags back into layer 1.** `load_card_tags` is scoped to
  `source='tagger_bulk'` for exactly this reason; keep it that way, or the two tasks become
  circular.
- Roles are assigned from card text and tags alone — **no deck context**. A card's role in a
  *specific* deck can differ (Skullclamp is card_draw generally, but a token deck's engine).
  If 6.6's quotas turn out to need it, that's a per-deck overlay on this table, not a change here.
- `MIN_ROLE_SCORE`/`SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE` were set from a handful of named cards, not swept. If a
  later task is sensitive to the secondary-role rate (11.7% of cards), sweep
  `SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE` against that rate rather than re-tuning per card.
