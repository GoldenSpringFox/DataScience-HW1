## Decisions
- **Scored, not first-match-wins.** Real cards carry several role signals (Path to Exile is tagged
  `ramp`, `tutor` *and* `spot-removal`), so each role accumulates a score, argmax wins, and the
  runner-up falls out as the secondary. Coarse scale: ~3–5 anchor, 0.5–1.0 corroborator, negative
  for a correction.
- **Anchor tags only, never the full ancestor expansion.** 5.5 expanded ancestors, so a wordy card
  like Cyclonic Rift carries ten `removal-<type>` tags — summing them would let verbosity beat
  meaning. Hierarchy verified empirically first, which caught two false assumptions:
  `counterspell` is *not* under `removal`, and `gives-trample` is *not* under `gives-evasion`.
- **Layers combine with max, not sum.** Summing double-counts — a draw spell tagged `draw` whose
  text also says "draw a card" is one piece of evidence seen twice. It showed: Mind Stone came out
  a draw spell. Max also keeps a one-anchor card comparable however it was seen.
- **Penalties apply *after* the layers combine**, not inside the tag layer. Ponder is the proof:
  `cantrip` + `hand-neutral` cancel its tag-layer `draw`, but the text layer independently reads
  "Draw a card." A negative weight is a claim about the **card**, not about a layer.
- **Tag conjunctions (`TAG_COMBOS`)** for what additive weights can't express: a lord is
  `board_presence`, but a lord that *also* grants evasion is a `wincon`. With plain weights the
  only way to promote Eldrazi Monument would be a `gives-evasion` weight that then fires on all
  ~1,000 evasion granters.
- **`land` is a type-line override**, with the functional role as secondary (Bojuka Bog → land /
  graveyard_hate) — a land that also ramps must not be counted out of the mana base.
- **Layer 1 is scoped to `source='tagger_bulk'`** so 6.5's mined tags can't feed back and make the
  two tasks circular.
- Fully deterministic — no seeds, no fitting. Ties broken by a declared `ROLE_PRIORITY`.

### The vocabulary, and how each question was settled
Every one of these was found by looking at classified cards, not by a failing test.

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Cantrips / self-cashing mana rocks — are they `draw`? | **No** (`cantrip`, `hand-neutral` penalties) |
| Loot / rummage are hand-neutral too? | **Yes, they are draw** — one card reaching several is advantage |
| "Ramp" reads wrong for Dark Ritual | Renamed **`mana_acceleration`** |
| Cost reducers? | **Yes** — paying less accelerates like producing more |
| Colour fixing? | **No.** Needed no code: Prismatic Omen carries `mana-fix` but no `ramp` |
| Land destruction — its own role? | **No.** `stax` when nothing is given back; `spot_removal` when the land is replaced or a nonland could have been hit |
| Mass land destruction? | **`stax`** — Armageddon and Winter Orb do the same thing to a game |
| Is a lord a `wincon`? | **No, `board_presence`** — unless it grants evasion or scales |
| One-shot board buffs (Overrun)? | **`wincon`** |
| Extra turns / combats? | **`wincon`.** Extra upkeeps / end steps are **`enhancer`** — value, not a clock |
| Damage aimed at players? | **`wincon`** (`burn-creature` is the discriminator keeping Lightning Bolt in removal) |
| Should every creature be `board_presence`? | **No.** Token makers, lords, counter distributors, and big bodies (power/toughness ≥5) only |
| Generic equipment (Bonesplitter)? | **Not** board presence |
| Pillowfort — `defensive` or `stax`? | **`defensive`**, stax secondary |
| Does `protection` cover protecting *you*? | **No, your permanents only** — so Solitary Confinement is `defensive` |
| Granting "can't be countered"? | **`stack_interaction`** |
| The leftover payoff/multiplier group in `other`? | New role **`enhancer`** — proposed by the data, named by the user |
| Precedence for stax *and* wipe *and* graveyard hate? | **stax > boardwipe > graveyard_hate** |
| How liberal should secondaries be? | **Liberal** — `SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE` 1.5 → 1.0 |
| Whispersilk Cloak, Teferi's Protection | **Accepted as genuine 50/50s**, not fixed |
| `synergy_piece` as a default | **Dropped.** It named nothing for 12,841 cards; `other` is the only default |

## Bugs
- **A lone 0.5 corroborator could become a primary role, and no test could have caught it.** The
  spot-check passed at 98% while `wincon` held **843** cards against the ~190 its anchors cover —
  `power-boost-to-all` was making every anthem a wincon just by being the only role that scored.
  Fixed with `MIN_ROLE_SCORE = 1.0`; wincon dropped 843 → 150. The bug only affected cards with
  *no* strong signal, and every card in a labeled set of famous cards has one.
- **Three Tagger tags mean something other than their name** — the recurring trap of this task:
  - `mass-land-denial` is about *denial*, not destruction (Winter Orb, Blood Moon, Stasis). Using
    it as the MLD anchor made Blood Moon mass land destruction.
  - `removal-land` also sits on every "destroy target permanent" card. The discriminator is the
    *other* `removal-<type>` tags: Stone Rain carries only `removal-land`; Acidic Slime carries
    three more.
  - `tax` is not stax — Tagger uses it for "something happens when an opponent does something",
    covering Soul Warden and Roaming Throne. Demoted; `cast-tax`/`cost-increaser` are the real
    anchors.

  General rule: *before using a Tagger tag as a role anchor, list the cards that carry it without
  the tag you assume co-occurs with it.*
- **A signal added to the wrong layer silently broke the agreement metric.** `CREATURE_SCORE`
  initially lived in `score_text`, so the "heuristic layer" fired on every creature and agreement
  fell 80.4% → 62.9% — not because classification got worse. Fixed by applying type-line signals
  in `classify` alongside the `land` override; recovered to 76.6%. A metric over "two independent
  layers" stops measuring that the moment a signal lands in the wrong one.
- **Lowering a threshold re-audits every corroborator weight.** Dropping `SECONDARY_MIN_SCORE` to
  1.0 gave all 5,219 cards carrying `removal-creature` a spurious `boardwipe` secondary (2,597 of
  them). Only the pool-wide secondary count caught it.
- Two regexes double-scored the same clause (`gains?` and a redundant `gain`), pushing Craterhoof
  Behemoth from `wincon` to `evasion`. Now covered by exact-score tests.
- Necropotence classified as `graveyard_hate` from "exile that card from **your** graveyard" —
  hitting your own resource is a cost, never hate. Rules now carry negative lookaheads on "your".
- One mapped tag (`static-ability`) didn't exist. `unknown_tags()` runs on every build for exactly
  this — a typo'd slug reads like a rule but contributes nothing.

## Outcome
Done, 55 tests (460 passing package-wide). The held-out spot-check reached 150/150, **which should
be distrusted, and the notebook says so**: after three rounds, 92 of the 150 labels were written
from the user's own instructions, so it measures "was the instruction implemented" far more than
"does this generalise." The honest checks are the distribution table and the card grids.

Final distribution highlights: `other` 6,950 · `board_presence` 4,945 · `spot_removal` 3,607 ·
`draw` 2,975 · `mana_acceleration` 1,734 · … · `enhancer` 309. **33.6% of cards carry a secondary**;
layer agreement 74.9% over the 13,166 cards both layers read.

`notebooks/roles_exploration.ipynb` is the review surface — `find()`/`show()` filtering,
`explain(card)` showing the exact tags and regexes that fired with their weights, and four
misclassification hunters (`disagreements`, `borderline`, `thin_evidence`, `audit_tag`). All three
misleading-tag traps above were found with `audit_tag`.
