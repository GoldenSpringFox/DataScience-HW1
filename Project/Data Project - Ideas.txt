~~~ MTG Project - Initial Idea ~~~

Problem Domain: Magic the Gathering

1. Problem Domain and Potential Questions

Main Problem: It is difficult to cut cards from decks to make room for new ones

Potential Questions: 
- How can we identify common deck building patterns per commander
- can we identify outliers / less synergistic cards that could be cut
- can we suggest context-appropriate cuts to not reduce important categories below certain thresholds

Project Direction:
- give an overview analysis on a deck, comparing to others in its category and evaluating important metrics (draw, ramp, disruption, win-conditions..)
- suggest cards to cut from the deck
- look in the database for existing cards that could work nicely in the deck

2. Data Collection Methods

mainly use existing databases:
- (card database)	https://scryfall.com/docs/api
- (deck database)	https://archidekt.com/		
- (deck database)	https://moxfield.com/
- (community tags)	https://tagger.scryfall.com/

3. Success Criteria
- given a deck with an obvious missing part, the program will suggest it
- given no specific directions, offer suggestions similar to EDHREC
- offer to cut cards that make sense in my personal experience (domain expert)
- identify different pattern for specific predetermined outlier commanders
